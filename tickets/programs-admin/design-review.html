The product manager built a prototype of the admin sign-ups page. We want a designer's view of two parts of it: the waitlist flow and the summary tiles. Play with it, try to break it, and find where it's confusing or slow. Feel free to prototype your own version of anything that feels wrong.

Keep the admin side simple. It's built in Retool. Before you invest in an idea, ask Ishi or Ken whether Retool can do it, and wait for a go. If it's not a clear go, stay with what Retool does by default.

Design the event page for each registration state Design Kadet app

After an admin approves a Kadet, the Kadet is asked to confirm or decline their spot, and waitlisted or rejected Kadets need to know where they stand. The event page needs a design for each of these states.

Keep it simple. We only want custom screens where the Kadet has to act or where plain text won't do. If you think something is really needed, say so and we'll build it. If you think we can drop something, say that too.
