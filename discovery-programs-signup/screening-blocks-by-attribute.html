Screening Blocks, by Attribute

Instead of five brand columns, blocks key off what an instance of a program actually is. A brand name is just a label for whatever bundle of attributes that run happens to have — it doesn't drive the form directly anymore.

Core Profile & Consent
Always on, regardless of attributes
General fields — same shape for every program
  • First name, last name, active email address
  • Phone number, province, school, year level
  • Demographic and household context
  • Data privacy consent (RA 10173 / Data Privacy Act)
▶
Motivation & Fit
Why they're applying, ranked reasons
Triggers on Competitive OR Multi-Week
  • Where did you hear about this program?
  • Why did you decide to participate? (select all that apply)
  • Rank your top reasons for joining
  • Why do you want to join this specific program?
▶
Self-Assessment
Confidence or knowledge rating scale
Triggers on Multi-Week OR Has Deliverable — see the gap flagged below
  • Rate your confidence in the skills this program requires (1–5)
  • Rate your knowledge of the program's topic (1–5)
  • Have you had real-world experience in this area before?
  • How familiar are you with [topic]? (0–10)
  • How familiar are you with our organization? (0–10)
  • Do you know where to access relevant career resources?
▶
Eligibility Screening
Gating questions and supporting materials
Triggers on Competitive OR Sponsor Attached
  • Upload supporting materials (resume, portfolio, or work sample)
  • Will this be your first formal experience of this kind?
  • What qualities do you have that would help you succeed, solo or in a group?
  • Have you reviewed the program handbook or guidelines?
▶
Partner Data Consent
Sharing contact/resume with sponsors
Triggers on Sponsor Attached
  • Agree to program-specific terms (privacy notice, NDA, output ownership)
  • Opt in to a formal internship or role with a partner company
  • Permission to share your resume and contact details with partner companies
▶
Commitment Agreement
Formal sign-off on program expectations
Triggers on Multi-Week OR Has Deliverable
  • Formal agreement to fulfill program expectations and responsibilities

Centrally Run doesn't trigger any block above, and that's likely correct. It isn't really a content attribute like the other four — it's a governance one. It probably determines who's allowed to toggle blocks on or off for a given instance, not which blocks show up. That reframes Hub Events: its earlier "varies" status wasn't a content problem, it was this attribute — decentralized control, not decentralized content.

Worked examples

Plugging real and hypothetical instances into the rules above, to pressure-test whether the four content attributes actually predict the right blocks.

VirApp Cohort
Competitive Sponsor Multi-Week Deliverable Central

AI Skills Workshop
Competitive Sponsor Multi-Week Deliverable Central
Predicted: no blocks beyond Core Profile.
But the real Workshops form did include Self-Assessment for exactly this kind of session. The four attributes miss it — pointing to a likely fifth content attribute: whether the session teaches a specific skill in depth.
Resume Refinement Workshop
Competitive Sponsor Multi-Week Deliverable Central
Predicted: no blocks beyond Core Profile. This one, correctly.
Same attribute profile, different real-world need. The AI workshop and the resume-refinement workshop score identically on all five attributes, yet only one actually wants a self-assessment. That's the clearest evidence the attribute set isn't complete yet — worth testing "teaches a specific skill" as a sixth attribute with program managers before this goes further.
